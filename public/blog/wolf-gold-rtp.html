Wolf Gold RTP, Jackpots & Volatility (Free Demo Guide)

July 2026 · LaJugadaSegura00

Wolf Gold is a long-running Pragmatic Play favourite, built around a Money Respin feature and three fixed jackpots. Here's its RTP, volatility and how the jackpots actually work.

Wolf Gold RTP The standard RTP is 96.01% — right around the industry norm. It's a long-run average, not a session figure, and lower-RTP versions exist, so check the game info panel. For the basics, see what RTP means.

Volatility: medium Wolf Gold is medium volatility, which makes it steadier than high-variance slots like Gates of Olympus. Wins come a bit more regularly, with the standout moments concentrated in the respin feature and jackpots rather than huge base-game hits.

How the jackpots and respins work On a 5x3 grid with 25 paylines, landing six or more Money symbols triggers the Money Respin feature: those symbols lock and you get three respins, with each new Money symbol resetting the respins. Fill enough positions and you win the Mega Jackpot; smaller Major and Mini jackpots pay for partial fills. There's also a full-moon feature that turns full stacks of wilds, plus regular free spins. The Mega Jackpot is a fixed multiple of your bet, not a progressive that grows over time.

Max win Outside the jackpots, the standard max win is around 2,500x your stake. As with any slot, that top figure is a rare outcome rather than something to plan around.
