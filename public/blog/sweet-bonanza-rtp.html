Sweet Bonanza RTP, Max Win & How the Tumble Feature Works

July 2026 · LaJugadaSegura00

Sweet Bonanza is the candy-themed Pragmatic Play slot known for its tumbling wins and bright multiplier bombs. Here's its RTP, volatility and max win, plus how the core features actually work.

Sweet Bonanza RTP The standard RTP is 96.51%. As always, that's an average measured over an enormous number of spins, not a guide to one session — and some casinos offer reduced-RTP versions, so check the in-game info panel. See what RTP means if you want the full picture.

Volatility: high Sweet Bonanza is high volatility. Wins can be infrequent, but the feature rounds carry the potential for large multiplier-driven payouts. That pattern rewards patience and a bankroll big enough to ride out quiet spells — not chasing losses with bigger bets.

How the tumble feature works Sweet Bonanza uses pay-anywhere scatter pays on a 6x5 grid: eight or more matching candies anywhere pay, then tumble away so new symbols can drop in for chained wins. During free spins, multiplier bombs worth 2x up to 100x land on the reels and add together when they hit a win. Four or more lollipop scatters trigger the free spins round, and an optional "ante bet" raises your stake to double the chance of triggering it.

Max win Sweet Bonanza's max win is a huge 21,100x your stake — one of the higher caps among popular slots. It's also extraordinarily rare, so read it as a headline number rather than a realistic target.
