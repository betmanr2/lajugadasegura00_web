Sugar Rush RTP, Cluster Pays & Volatility Explained

July 2026 · LaJugadaSegura00

Sugar Rush is a Pragmatic Play cluster-pays slot with a candy grid and sticky multiplier spots that build up as you play. Here's its RTP, volatility and how the cluster mechanic works.

Sugar Rush RTP The standard RTP is 96.50%. It's a long-run average rather than a session guarantee, and some operators run lower-RTP versions, so check the in-game info panel before playing. If RTP is new to you, start with what RTP means.

Volatility: high Sugar Rush is high volatility. Returns can be uneven, with the meaningful wins arriving through multiplier build-up and the free spins rather than the base game. Bet sizing matters here — see high vs low volatility slots.

How cluster pays work Instead of paylines, Sugar Rush uses a 7x7 grid with cluster pays: land five or more matching symbols touching horizontally or vertically and they pay, then tumble away for new symbols to drop in. Each grid position that forms a win becomes a multiplier spot, and every subsequent win on that same spot increases its multiplier (up to 128x), which can stack across the board. Free spins carry those multiplier spots over between spins, which is where big rounds come from.

Max win The max win is 5,000x your stake — a rare ceiling reached only when multiple high multiplier spots line up during the feature. Read it as the edge case it is.
