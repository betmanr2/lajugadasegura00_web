Gates of Olympus RTP, Volatility & Max Win (Free Demo)

July 2026 · LaJugadaSegura00

Gates of Olympus is one of Pragmatic Play's most recognisable slots, built around Zeus, tumbling symbols and multipliers that can land anywhere on the grid. This guide covers its RTP, volatility and realistic max win — the numbers that actually matter before you spin.

Gates of Olympus RTP The default RTP is 96.50%. That figure is a long-run statistical average across millions of spins, not a promise for any single session. One important catch: some operators run lower-RTP versions of the same game (down to around 94% or 95%), so always check the RTP listed inside the game's info panel before playing. If you're new to this number, our guide on what RTP means explains it in plain terms.

Volatility: high Gates of Olympus is a high-volatility slot. In practice that means long stretches with little return, broken up by rarer, much larger payouts. It's a poor fit for a small bankroll played at high stakes — the dry spells can empty a balance before a big hit ever arrives. Our guide on high vs low volatility slots explains how to size your bets around this.

How it works Instead of paylines, Gates of Olympus uses a pay-anywhere (scatter pays) system on a 6x5 grid: land eight or more matching symbols anywhere and they pay, then winning symbols tumble away and new ones drop in for potential chain wins. Multiplier orbs from 2x up to 500x can appear and add together, and four or more scatters trigger free spins where those multipliers become far more valuable.

Max win The maximum win is capped at 5,000x your stake. That is genuinely rare — the kind of outcome that makes highlight clips precisely because most sessions never come close. Treat it as the ceiling, not the expectation.
