How we choose what makes this list

Every offer we feature needs to clear three checks before it's added: a wagering requirement of 40x or lower, a clear expiry window disclosed up front, and a licensed operator verifiable through a public regulator registry. Offers that fail any of the three don't make the cut, regardless of headline bonus size.

What to check before you claim any offer, here or elsewhere

  • Wagering requirement and which games count toward it
  • Minimum deposit needed to unlock the bonus
  • Time limit to clear the requirement
  • Maximum bet size allowed while the bonus is active
  • Maximum withdrawal cap on bonus winnings

Free spins vs cash bonuses

Free spins are usually restricted to one specific slot and often carry their own separate, sometimes higher, wagering requirement on any winnings. A smaller cash bonus with flexible game choice and a lower multiple can be worth more in practice than a headline "200 free spins" offer — always compare the real requirement, not the number in the headline.

Frequently asked questions

Are welcome bonuses actually worth it?

Only if the wagering requirement and game weighting are reasonable. A big headline bonus at 50x can be worse than a smaller one at 20x.

What should I check before claiming a bonus?

The wagering multiplier, the maximum cashout, game contribution, minimum deposit, and the expiry period.
